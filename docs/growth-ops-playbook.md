# StayOnStay Growth + Ops Playbook

## Supply (10 hotels manual onboarding)
- Build a target sheet with 10 nearby medical-stay hotels, owner name, phone, and WhatsApp.
- Onboarding script: profile setup, room types, base prices, photos, and first 5 room numbers.
- Completion target: each onboarded hotel has at least 2 room types and 1 image per type.

## Demand (5 hospitals outreach)
- Outreach list: patient coordination desks and social work teams for 5 hospitals.
- One-pager handout: "Book verified stays near treatment center in under 60 seconds".
- Follow-up cadence: day 2 and day 7 through WhatsApp and phone.

## Distribution (WhatsApp admin loop)
- Template 1: welcome + onboarding video + login link.
- Template 2: low inventory reminder and how to update prices.
- Template 3: weekly occupancy check-in and image quality checklist.

## SEO guardrails
- Create a page only if inventory exists: `stay-near-<hospital>-<city>`.
- Include real room prices, real availability, and direct booking CTA.
